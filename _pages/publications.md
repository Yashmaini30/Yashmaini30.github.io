## 📄 IEEE Conference Papers

### 1. **Xception for Tomato Leaf Disease Detection: Hyperparameter Tuning and Fine-Tuning Approaches**  
**Yash Maini, Sanjay Kumar Singh, Pranshu Saxena**  
📍 *IEEE ICAIQSA 2024 — Nagpur, India*  
🔗 [DOI](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10882346) | [GitHub](https://github.com/Yashmaini30/Tomato_Leaf_Disease_Detection) | [Blog](https://xception-model-for-leaf-diseases.hashnode.dev/xception-based-tomato-disease-detection-9723-9723-accuracy)

- Achieved **97.23% accuracy** using an optimized **Xception model**.
- Combined hyperparameter tuning and fine-tuning for enhanced precision.
- Applied transfer learning to automate agricultural leaf disease detection.

### 2. **Breast Tumor Classification with Fine-Tuned Hyperparameter Training Using Deep Learning Models**  
**Yash Maini, Sanjay Kumar Singh, Pranshu Saxena**  
📍 *IEEE AdSoc 5.0 2025 — Kolkata, India*  
🔗 [DOI](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10963186) | [GitHub](https://github.com/Yashmaini30/Breast-Cancer-Detection) | [Blog](https://deep-learning-for-breast-cancer.hashnode.dev/discover-ais-role-in-breast-cancer-detection-deep-and-transfer-learning-achieves-9846-accuracy)

- Achieved **98.46% classification accuracy** on mammogram data using fine-tuned CNNs.
- Applied **Population-Based Training** for hyperparameter optimization.
- Addressed early detection challenges in breast cancer screening.
